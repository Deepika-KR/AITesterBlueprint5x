Assumptions & Open Questions

  1. What are the approved QA and pre-production URLs, build identifiers, supported browser/device versions, and non-production accounts?
  2. What are the password rules, exact validation/error messages, authentication failure policy, and rate-limit/lockout thresholds?
  3. What should remember-me persist, for how long, and what are the configured session idle/absolute timeout values?
  4. What reset-token validity, expiry, reuse, and password-change rules should be verified?
  5. Which 2FA, enterprise SSO, and social login providers are enabled for this release? These conditional flows are excluded until configuration is confirmed.
  6. What exact load profile, standard connection profile, and measurement method define the login page-load target of under 2 seconds?
  7. What are the approved assistive-technology combinations and WCAG 2.1 AA acceptance process?

TBD items are intentionally not replaced with assumed product behavior. Resolve relevant questions before test execution or case sign-off.
